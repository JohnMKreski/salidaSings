COME SING WITH US!

The local women’s community chorus, The Noteables, is seeking singers for our Christmas concert series, Carry the Light, scheduled for December 11-13.

The chorus will practice every Thursday night from 5:30-7:30 p.m. starting September 3, at Salida United Methodist Church, on the corner of 4th and D Streets. No auditions are required.


Under the animated direction of Linda Taylor, the chorus will perform a variety of seasonal music, including unusual arrangements of Christmas carols, winter ballads, humorous selections and songs of peace, love and light.



Songs include the familiar Sussex Carol; Hot Chocolate, a fun jazzy salsa-style tune about our favorite holiday hot drink; and O Holy Night/Silent Night, a partner song that weaves together the two adored Christmas carols. The program continues with Bring A Torch Jeanette, Isabella and Gather Round, capturing the Christmas story with a bouncy Caribbean flavor. Carry the Light and Christmas Peace spread serenity, hope and joy. Plus, many more!


The concerts will feature guest instrumentalists Oso Blanco on drums, Kathleen Chiras on electric bass, and Marnie Knapp on hammered dulcimer.


Dues: Sliding scale: $30.00-75.00, with financial assistance available from our treasury.


Absences: 4 absences allowed (Please... not all in a row)


Dress rehearsals: Tuesdays, December 1 and 8


Joining The Noteables will be the most rewarding and uplifting decision you’ve made in a long time. Please join us. Don’t be shy. You’ll love it! Music is healing and brings perfect strangers together in laughter, joy, and harmony, Remember that singing in a choir is cheaper than therapy, healthier than drinking, and more fun than working out. Come Carry the Light with us.



For more information and to sign up call Linda Taylor, 719 539-2428.
