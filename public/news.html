We Can't Stop Singing!

The local women’s community chorus, The Noteables, is preparing for their winter concert series, "Carry the Light" scheduled for December 11-13.


Under the animated direction of Linda Taylor, The Noteables will perform a wide variety of seasonal music, including unusual arrangements of Christmas carols, winter ballads, humorous selections and songs of serenity, peace, love and light.



Songs will include: Joy Hallelujah, a partner song combining Joy To the World with the Hallelujah Chorus; the familiar Bring A Torch Jeanette, Isabella; Hot Chocolate, a fun, jazzy salsa-style tune about our favorite holiday hot drink; and Holy Night, Silent Night, a partner song, combining the two beloved carols.

The program continues with Gather Round, capturing the Christmas story with a bouncy Caribbean flavor and a Celtic version of Shepherd’s Joy. Carry the Light encourages us to keep our lights strong and the birth of Jesus is woven into Dona, Nobis, Pacem and Away In a Manger. Plus more!



The chorus will be accompanied by Kurt Wipperfurth on drums and Kate Chiras on electric bass. Featured will be Marnie Knapp playing several instrumental carols on her hammered dulcimer.



Concert dates:

Date Time Venue Location
Fri, December 11 7:00 PM Mountain Heights Church Buena Vista
Sat, December 12 7:00 PM Salida United Methodist Church Salida
Sun, December 13 3:00 PM Salida United Methodist Church Salida



Remember that music is healing and has the ability to bring perfect strangers together in laughter, joy and harmony, something that The Noteables are famous for! Join us during these difficult times for a concert series that will lighten your heart and fill you with the Christmas spirit. Come carry the light with us.
