Come Sing With Us!

The Noteables are recruiting female singers for their spring concert series, “Circles of Life”, May 1-3. No auditions required. Thursday night rehearsals start January 22, 5:30-7:30 pm at Salida United Methodist Church.


Under the animated direction of Linda Taylor, the chorus will perform a wide variety of music that reflects the themes of stepping into new circles of life, taking risks, finding positive ways to rediscover ourselves and embracing the opportunity to begin anew.



Songs will include the jazzy Theme from New York, New York; You and I Can Change the World; Gershwin’s They All Laughed which pokes fun at the fears that hold us back; the contemplative Homeward Bound; Cindy Lauper’s Time After Time; Crossing A Bridge from Anastasia; Always Look On the Bright Side of Life from Spamalot and the hilarious, Alto’s Lament. Plus lots more!

Concert dates:

  • Friday, May 1, Mountain Heights Church, BV,
  • Saturday, May 2, Salida Methodist Church and Sunday,
  • Sunday, May 3, Salida SteamPlant

Dress rehearsals: Tuesday, April 21 and Monday, April 28

Dues: Sliding scale $30.00-$75.00 per season.

Absences: 4 allowed, (but not all back to back).



The Noteables are a chorus consisting of 20-30 women. We are a compassionate, friendly and supportive group of singers. We cheer each other on, help out when someone gets sick and have awesome rehearsals, learning vocal technique while having fun. Let’s face it. If it isn’t fun why do it? Please join us. Don’t be shy. You’ll love it!!
